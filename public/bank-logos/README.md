# Iranian bank logos

The SVG logos in `banks/` were copied from the user-provided `logos-main.zip` archive and are used directly in the bank picker, account cards, and card watermark. The archive did not include a license or attribution file.

The account form automatically selects the matching bank when a card prefix is recognized. Prefixes for the banks listed by the user are maintained from that supplied list; remaining mappings are best-effort and informed by public references including the [zod-ir bank prefix list](https://github.com/Reza-kh80/zod-ir/blob/bfb5a862d06add5fb5a46418b738270d974cefbd/src/data/constants.ts), [Laravel Iranian Banks directory](https://github.com/lokiwich/laravel-iranian-banks/blob/main/database/data/banks.php), and [DNTPersianUtils](https://github.com/VahidN/DNTPersianUtils.Core/blob/95898c44ddd07a0dd46fc3bb32bcba1889accbef/src/DNTPersianUtils.Core/Providers/IranianBankCardProviders.cs). Prefix detection is a best-effort bank-name check, not authoritative card issuer verification. Public lists disagree on some prefixes; the account form does not auto-select a bank for Middle East Bank or Iran-Venezuela Bank until a reliable mapping is available.
